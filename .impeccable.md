# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users

Engineers and platform/security staff at Sorva Studios, working inside an
internal control plane for a GitHub org (~350 repos) and a production AWS
account. They open it to answer "is anything wrong, and can I fix it now" —
usually in a hurry, often between other work. Roni (owner) and a system
administrator hold the elevated permissions; everyone else reads.

The job: see compliance posture at a glance, drill into what is failing, and
act. SOC-2 evidence is the backdrop, so the interface should feel trustworthy
and precise, never toy-like.

### Brand Personality

**Confident · precise · alive.**

The app should feel like a professional instrument that is switched on — not a
form, and not a corporate dashboard. Calm when everything passes, visibly
urgent when it does not.

### Aesthetic Direction

The app was originally styled as a GitHub clone: Inter, a GitHub-derived
palette (`gh-blue #0969da`, `gh-green #1a7f37`, `gh-red #cf222e`), grey cards,
thin borders. That styling is deliberately quiet and is the reason pages have
read as flat and unfinished.

**Agreed direction (2026-08-10):** break out of it. The navbar stays as-is;
page interiors get a stronger identity, starting with the AWS tab and rolling
outward. Explicitly endorsed: **saturated colour, depth and layering, and
motion**. No constraints were placed on how far to push.

Reference: Vanta's control dashboard — summary cards with a percentage and a
progress bar, a filter row, and a wide table with two-line cells. Take its
information design; do not take its restraint.

Anti-references, learned the hard way across four rejected attempts:
- flat grey-on-white tables with thin borders — reads as unfinished
- sparse editorial layouts with lots of whitespace and small grey text
- anything that looks visually unrelated to the rest of the app's chrome
- native OS form controls sitting next to custom-styled ones

Both light and dark must work. Light is the default.

### Design Principles

1. **Posture is the loudest thing on the page.** State — how many checks are
   failing — should be readable across a room, and colour should follow it.
   The page looks different when something is wrong.
2. **Colour carries meaning, never decoration.** Green, amber and red mean
   pass, attention and fail. Nothing else competes for saturation.
3. **Depth shows structure.** Elevation and layering communicate hierarchy;
   thin borders alone do not.
4. **Motion confirms, never entertains.** Entrances orient, transitions
   connect views, values animate to their result. Nothing loops.
5. **Precision under the drama.** Resource identifiers stay monospace and
   exact; numbers are tabular; the loud parts never make the data harder to
   read.
